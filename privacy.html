Privacy policy

Shihab — private personal assistant of Taher Fouda · last updated 3 October 2026

Who uses Shihab

Shihab is a private application used by one person only: its owner, Taher Fouda. It is not available to the public.

What Google data Shihab accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): to search and summarise Taher's email for him. Shihab cannot send, delete or modify email.
  • Google Drive and Google Slides (read-only): to find and read Taher's documents when he asks.
  • Google Calendar: to show Taher's schedule; events are created, changed or deleted only after Taher explicitly instructs and confirms each change.

How the data is used

Google data is used only to provide features to Taher himself. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with or transferred to third parties, and not used to train general-purpose AI models. To answer a request, relevant excerpts may be processed by the AI service that powers Shihab under that provider's API terms, solely to produce the answer for Taher.

Storage and security

Shihab runs on Taher's private server. Access credentials are stored only in that server's configuration and are never published. Only Taher can use Shihab; messages from anyone else are not treated as instructions.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Shihab's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact Taher Fouda at the support email shown on Shihab's Google sign-in screen.
