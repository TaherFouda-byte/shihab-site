Shihab

The private personal assistant of Taher Fouda

Shihab is a private, single-user assistant built for and operated by Taher Fouda. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

With Taher's permission, Shihab reads his own Google account (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar and Google Slides) in order to answer his questions, summarise his messages and documents, and show his schedule. Calendar events are only created or changed when Taher explicitly asks and confirms. It can also read his own WHOOP and Garmin fitness data to give him private health and training summaries.
